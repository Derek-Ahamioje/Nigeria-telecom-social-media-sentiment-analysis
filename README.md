# Nigeria Telecom Social Media Sentiment Analysis

## Project Overview

This project evaluates customer sentiment and social media engagement across Nigeria's four largest telecommunications providers: MTN Nigeria, Globacom, Airtel Nigeria, and 9mobile.

The study applies Twitter analytics, Tableau visualisation, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) topic modelling to uncover customer perceptions, engagement trends, and service satisfaction indicators.

---

## Business Problem

Telecommunications providers generate significant customer interactions through social media platforms. Understanding customer sentiment, engagement levels, and recurring discussion topics can help organisations improve customer experience, marketing effectiveness, and service delivery.

This project explores how social media analytics and machine learning techniques can be used to evaluate customer opinions and online engagement across major Nigerian telecom operators.

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Twitter/X

**Sample Size:**
- 5,000 tweets from MTN Nigeria
- 5,000 tweets from Globacom
- 5,000 tweets from Airtel Nigeria
- 5,000 tweets from 9mobile

The dataset includes tweet content, likes, replies, retweets, and engagement metrics.

---

## Methodology

### Data Collection

- Twitter data scraping using Twint
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Removal of duplicates and irrelevant records

### Exploratory Data Analysis

- Engagement analysis
- Likes and replies analysis
- Posting frequency assessment
- Telecom provider comparison

### Topic Modelling

Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) was applied to identify dominant discussion themes across customer conversations.

### Visual Analytics

Interactive Tableau dashboards were developed to visualise customer engagement patterns and sentiment trends.

---

## Key Findings

- Over 20,000 telecom-related tweets were analysed across four providers.
- MTN generated the highest customer engagement, recording 334,161 likes and 120,512 replies.
- Airtel produced relatively low engagement despite maintaining a high posting frequency.
- Promotional campaigns and giveaways generated the highest customer interaction levels.
- LDA topic modelling identified recurring themes related to customer support, promotions, service quality, and subscriber engagement.
- Word cloud analysis highlighted strong positive engagement around MTN promotional activities and recurring customer service concerns across providers.
